# CGIAR MEL and GeOC web GIS

[Back to case studies](README.md)

## Context

CGIAR’s Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) platform supported research reporting. GeOC added a web GIS for comparing land-management options across places with similar environmental and social conditions.

## What I worked on

- Led a five-person iMMAP team that contributed to the existing MEL platform and GeOC from 2016 to 2018.
- Worked on MEL forms and approval workflows that brought land-management data into the platform. Used Python and pandas to validate incoming data before it reached downstream reports.
- Worked with PHP and MySQL on MEL and with Java, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, and GeoServer on the geospatial side.

## Result

GeOC connected MEL users to maps and land-management records they could compare by local conditions. I co-authored public architecture and installation documentation for the two systems.
